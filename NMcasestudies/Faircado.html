Faircado · Resale & sustainability

50,000 monthly downloads and #13 on the German App Store

A fast-growing sustainable shopping app scaling in the German market.

The client

Faircado, a fast-growing sustainable shopping app operating in the German market.

The challenge

Accelerate user acquisition and brand awareness in the highly competitive German market.

The approach

  • Partnered as both advisor and investor, co-leading the TikTok marketing strategy.
  • High-volume content production across multiple accounts.
  • Onboarded top German creators.
  • Optimised organic social alongside the internal brand team to amplify visibility and strengthen the brand narrative.

The results

  • Over 50,000 monthly app downloads
  • #13 on the German App Store — a major milestone in the company's growth
  • A repeatable TikTok content engine run with the internal team
