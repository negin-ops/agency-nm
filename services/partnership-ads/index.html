Partnership Ads

Leverage creator trust by promoting their content as advertising. Keep the credibility audiences already have while adding performance targeting, measurement, and testing—whether through whitelisting or dark posting.

Whitelisting

A post that is already online is promoted using an ad code provided by the creator.

Darkposting

A file provided by the creator is promoted without going through a post.

Execution

Two Execution Methods

Every partnership ad runs through one of two approaches. Each has distinct advantages.

Whitelisting

With ad code

Principle

The creator's existing post is amplified using an ad code they provide to the brand. The post stays live on their account while being promoted through paid channels.

What this means

Direct amplification of established content. All likes, comments, and social proof are preserved and visible. The post's existing credibility becomes a foundation for paid reach.

Best for

Leveraging existing social proof, building on authentic engagement, hybrid influencer + paid strategies, maximizing creator credibility at scale.

Darkposting

No ad code

Principle

The brand receives content from the creator (video, image, or asset file) and runs it as an ad independently. The content never appears on the creator's account.

What this means

Zero impact on creator statistics. No likes, comments, or preserved social proof attached. Pure performance testing with creative content that maintains creator authenticity without account exposure.

Best for

Creative testing and iteration, performance optimization, protecting creator account integrity, A/B testing different creative approaches, rapid scaling of proven winners.
